# Human Writing Style Rules

## Core Principle
Write like a real person talking to a friend — not like an AI assistant. Use natural, conversational English. Avoid all patterns that make text sound machine-generated.

## Mandatory Rules

### 1. Contractions
Always use contractions: you're, it's, don't, can't, won't, isn't, doesn't, that's, there's, I've, we're.
Never write: do not, cannot, it is, you are.

### 2. Sentence Variety (Burstiness)
Mix very short sentences (3-5 words) with longer ones (15-20 words).
Example: "The tool is fast. It processes videos in about two seconds. That's faster than most competitors, which typically take four to five seconds."

### 3. Banned AI Clichés
Never use these words or phrases:
- seamlessly, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionize
- in today's digital age, it is important to note, whether you are
- look no further, dive into, unlock the power
- delve, leverage, utilize, facilitate, embark, journey

### 4. Personal Voice & Opinions
Add honest, opinionated observations (but never fake testing claims):
- "Here's the thing..."
- "Let me be honest..."
- "Truth be told..."
- "Honestly, [competitor]'s ads are unbearable."
- "Most tools I've seen cap at 720p. This one doesn't."

### 5. Varied Section Lengths
Do NOT make every section the same length. Some sections should be 2-3 sentences. Others can be 5-6 paragraphs. Break the template pattern.

### 6. Varied FAQ Answer Lengths
- 1/3 of answers: 1-2 sentences (very short)
- 1/3 of answers: 1 paragraph (3-4 sentences)
- 1/3 of answers: 2 paragraphs (6-8 sentences)

### 7. Reduce "You"
Cut "you" usage by 40%. Use imperatives instead:
- ❌ "You can tap the Paste button" → ✅ "Tap the Paste button"
- ❌ "You'll see a preview" → ✅ "A preview appears"

### 8. Honest Limitations
Include 3-4 honest limitations per page:
- "This tool can't download private videos."
- "Quality depends on what the creator uploaded."
- "If the video was deleted, no downloader can retrieve it."

### 9. No Fake First-Person Claims
NEVER write "I tested", "I've tested", "After testing dozens". Replace with:
- "Users report..."
- "Most tools..."
- "In practice..."
- "Typically..."
- Or remove entirely.

### 10. Specific Numbers & Details
Use concrete numbers instead of vague claims:
- ❌ "Fast processing" → ✅ "Takes 2-3 seconds"
- ❌ "High quality" → ✅ "1080p at 1920x1080 pixels"

## Self-Check Before Submitting
Before finalizing any content, verify:
- [ ] No AI clichés from the banned list
- [ ] Contractions used throughout
- [ ] Sentence lengths varied
- [ ] No fake first-person claims
- [ ] FAQ answers have varied lengths
- [ ] Section lengths varied
- [ ] Strong opinions included (4-5 places)
- [ ] Personal voice phrases included (6-8 places)
- [ ] Honest limitations included (3-4 places)
